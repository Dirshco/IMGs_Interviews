💡 IMG Interview Tips

⭐ STAR Method Guide

What is STAR?

A structured framework for answering behavioral questions. Every "Tell me about a time…" question should use this format.

The STAR Framework

S
SituationSet the context — describe the background briefly (1–2 sentences)
T
TaskWhat was your specific responsibility or challenge?
A
ActionWhat did YOU specifically do? (This is the longest part — 60%)
R
ResultWhat was the outcome? Quantify where possible. What did you learn?

💡 STAR Tips for IMGs

• Use real examples from your clinical training — even from your home country

• Focus on YOUR actions, not the team's

• Quantify results: "Blood sugar stabilized within 48 hours"

• Always end with what you learned — US programs value self-reflection

• Aim for 2–3 minutes per answer
